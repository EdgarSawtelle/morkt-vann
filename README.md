# Mørkt vann – en kriminalgåte i Bergen

A Norwegian-learning detective game set in rainy Bergen. You play Mark Harlan, an American private detective who barely speaks Norwegian, and you solve the case by reading, understanding and building Norwegian sentences.

**Play it here:** https://USERNAME.github.io/morkt-vann/

## What's inside
- 6 chapters, rising from **A2-1 to B1-3**, each with its own grammar focus and a "What you'll learn" screen
- Every Norwegian word is tappable: meaning, dictionary form, gender or verb forms, grammar notes, an example sentence and audio
- Bergen, Trøndelag and Oslo speech, with standard Bokmål forms shown for dialect words
- Sentence puzzles with word tiles or free typing, plus in-story reactions and hints when you get it wrong
- Case notebook, saved-word list, flashcards with spaced repetition, and a quiz after every chapter
- Designed for e-ink tablets (no animations, large text) but works on any phone or computer

## Tips
- Audio uses your device's text-to-speech. Install a Norwegian voice for the best result.
- Progress is saved in your browser. Use Settings → Export / Import to move it to another device.
- Everything is in one file (`index.html`) and works offline once loaded.
